New setup (first-time install or new cassette)

This sets the number of gears and walks you through setting the position of every gear. Put the bike on a stand so you can turn the cranks.

Step 1 of 4

Loosen the shift cable at the derailleur and make sure the chain is on the smallest cog.

Step 2 of 4

Moving the motor to its starting position.

Pull the shift cable finger-tight and tighten the anchor bolt.

Step 3 of 4

Use the Shift points card below. Start on gear 1 and adjust until the chain runs quietly on the cog. Then tap Gear +. Each new gear starts at the previous gear's position, so pull cable until it shifts and runs quietly. Repeat up to the top gear.

Every adjustment is saved on the shifter straight away.

Step 4 of 4

Done. Shift through all the gears with the remote while turning the cranks to check them. Make a backup in All gear positions so you can restore these settings later.

+ pulls cable, which moves the chain toward the bigger cogs. − releases cable, toward the smaller cogs. The ±5 buttons approach the new position from the opposite side to take up slack, just like the original app.

Settings

Quick shift

Hold a button to shift several gears at once.

Overshoot

The motor goes past each shift point and comes back after a short delay. This can help with a worn cassette or chain. Test it on the stand first.

All gear positions (backup and restore)

Reading shifts the derailleur through every gear, so do it on the stand while turning the cranks.

Maintenance

Change shifter cable

Loosen the cable at the derailleur or take the chain off first. The motor moves to the fully released position so you can swap the cable. When you tap Finished, it goes back to its saved position.

Pair remote

Firmware 1.x: the shifter restarts automatically, then you turn on the remote. Older firmware: the shifter turns off. Turn it on, then turn on the remote. When the remote's orange light flashes, pairing is done. This disconnects the page.
